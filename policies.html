MORROW & CO. / INFORMATION

Clear answers.
No guesswork.

These pages describe what is currently configured for the store. Where a business decision or provider setup is still pending, it is stated plainly.

01 / SHIPPING

Shipping is shown before payment.

Shipping options, destination availability, timing, and any shipping charge must be confirmed by the store’s configured checkout provider before an order is paid. The storefront does not promise free shipping or a guaranteed delivery date.

02 / RETURNS

Ask before sending anything back.

A final return window, eligible conditions, and refund process require the store owner’s decision before publication. Contact bahae6593@gmail.com with your order reference before returning an item; do not ship goods without written instructions.

03 / PRIVACY

Only use information needed to help.

The static storefront stores a local shopping bag in your browser. Payment details are handled by PayPal when payment is configured. Customer information entered in checkout is sent only to the configured payment backend; no card details are stored by Morrow & Co. The store owner must add a final jurisdiction-specific privacy notice before launch.

04 / TERMS

Product details should match the item.

Prices, availability, product details, and supplier references are maintained from the current catalog. A legally reviewed terms document, governing entity, tax treatment, and business address still require the store owner’s input before production launch.

05 / FAQ

A few useful answers.

Can I buy more than one product?

Yes. The cart and order-review page support multiple different products and quantities. Unified PayPal payment remains unavailable until the backend credentials and shipping/tax configuration are connected.

How do I change my quantity?

Use the plus and minus controls in the bag or on the checkout review page. The line total and subtotal recalculate immediately.

Where is payment handled?

Existing product-specific PayPal links remain available as a fallback. The new multi-item path is designed to create and capture one PayPal order through the server-side Worker.

06 / CONTACT

Let’s talk it through.

Support messages are intended for bahae6593@gmail.com. The automated form remains unavailable until its secure email service is configured.
